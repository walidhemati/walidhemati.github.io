## Problem

Compute-bound kernels hide nothing behind memory: the instruction count decides. That makes N-body a sharp test of what Python GPU compilers emit.

## Approach

- Tiling, unrolling and register blocking in Triton, Warp, Numba and CUDA C; a packed FP16x2 variant.
- SASS counts per interaction explain the gap to the peak (issue bound ≈ 75 %).
- Validation: leapfrog order 2, Hermite order 4, Kepler period to 9.4e-6, Plummer energy to 1.2e-6.
- Head-to-head with the CUDA Samples nbody (GPU Gems 3), built unchanged.
