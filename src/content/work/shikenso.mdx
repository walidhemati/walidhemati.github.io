## The job

<p class="lede">You can only measure what you managed to record.</p>

Shikenso measures brand exposure in sports broadcasts and live streams. I joined in September 2024 on the computer-vision side, and since 2025 most of my work has been the systems around the detector: getting the video in, keeping the recording services alive, and joining the results with audience numbers.

## What I built

- **YOLOv8 in production.** I wrote the YOLOv8 tracker for box and polygon (segmentation) models, plugged it into batch processing and covered it with tests. Detection got roughly twice as fast, and that tracker is still the base of the detector today. I also built training and evaluation data for the logo models: VIA-to-YOLO polygon conversion, annotated evaluation sets and annotation QA tools.
- **A live-TV recording system, from the first commit.** About 50 channels in three countries, recorded in rolling 30-minute segments, with automatic failover between sources, programme tagging from the EPG guide, ffmpeg repair and joining, and a FastAPI scheduler with a web UI. It has a suite of about 900 tests.
- **30+ capture integrations** for broadcasters, streaming services and sports platforms across Europe, Australia and the Middle East. They feed the detector.
- **Audience data.** A TV-ratings platform that brings 10 audience-measurement sources from three countries into one place, and a rebuilt panel-audience service that runs about 22× faster.
- **Running it.** 36 scheduled capture services in production through Jenkins and systemd, containers on Kubernetes with Helm, Sentry for incidents, and regression tests for every fix.
