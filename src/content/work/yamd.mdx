## Overview

Molecular dynamics moves atoms forward in time by computing the forces between them and integrating Newton's equations of motion, step after step. The equations fit on a napkin. The bugs hide in the details: a sign in a force, an off-by-one in a neighbour loop, a time step that quietly drifts the energy.

yamd is my coursework for the University of Freiburg course Molecular Dynamics in C++. It started from the course's Meson template, which brought the build setup, Eigen and GoogleTest.

## How it is built

- **Eigen** for vectors and arrays, so the physics reads as linear algebra rather than index arithmetic.
- **GoogleTest** for unit tests, so each piece of the integrator can be checked on its own.
- **Meson** as the build system, from the course template.

The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/walidhemati/yamd).
