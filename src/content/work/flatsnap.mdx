## Problem

<p class="lede">The documents people scan are the ones they least want to share.</p>

A phone is the most convenient scanner most people own. The things that get scanned are rental contracts, letters from the authorities and medical forms.

Flatsnap reads them where they were photographed: on the phone.

## Constraints

- **Phone hardware only.** Recognition has to run on the device it was photographed with.
- **No server in the loop.** Reading the text does not depend on sending the page anywhere.
- **A published privacy policy.** The app's data handling is written down in a privacy policy that ships with its store listing.
