## Problem

<p class="lede">Tax paperwork is a year-long chore that ends in one stressful week.</p>

The receipts that matter for a German tax return arrive all year: invoices from tradespeople, bills for household services. By filing season they are scattered across drawers, inboxes and photo rolls, and the rules that decide what counts are spread across the Income Tax Act and the Fiscal Code.

The data involved is also some of the most private there is. SteuerApp starts from a simple position: none of it needs to leave the phone, and by default none of it does.

## Constraints

- **Offline by default.** OCR and tax classification run on the device without network access. An optional AI check, off by default and using the user's own API key, sends only the extracted receipt text, never images.
- **Encrypted at rest.** Records live in a local Room database encrypted with SQLCipher.
- **Works offline.** Recording a receipt cannot depend on a connection.
- **German tax law, as written.** The rules come from the statutes: the income tax tariff in §32a EStG, the reduction for household services and tradespeople in §35a EStG, and the filing deadlines of the Abgabenordnung (AO).
- **The output has to be usable.** The PDF follows the fields of ELSTER, the tax authority's filing portal, so the numbers can be carried over directly.

## Approach

The app is written in Kotlin for Android. A receipt goes through four steps, and by default every one of them runs on the phone.

## Decisions

<div class="decisions">
  <Decision chose="On-device OCR" over="a cloud OCR service">
    Receipt images never leave the phone, and recognition keeps working without a connection. The privacy promise is a property of the architecture, not a line in a policy.
  </Decision>
  <Decision chose="Rules shipped in the app" over="rules fetched from a server">
    The rules work fully offline. The trade-off is explicit: when the law changes, the rules change with an app update.
  </Decision>
  <Decision chose="A PDF mapped to ELSTER" over="filing from inside the app">
    The app prepares the numbers; filing stays with the user, in ELSTER. That keeps filing, and the user's ELSTER credentials, outside the app.
  </Decision>
</div>
