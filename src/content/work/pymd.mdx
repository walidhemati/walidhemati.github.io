## Problem

pymd is the Python successor of [yamd](/work/yamd/), my C++ molecular dynamics coursework. It reproduces the LAMMPS `in.lj` log and NIST reference energies, and asks how close Python gets to the established GPU MD codes.

## Approach

- Lennard-Jones and Gupta EAM (gold), velocity Verlet, Berendsen and Langevin thermostats.
- Neighbour lists rebuilt on the GPU; fused list-walk and force kernels.
- Validation: LAMMPS `in.lj` step 0 exact, NIST SRSW energies within 0.06 %, Johnson–Zollweg–Gubbins equation of state.
- LAMMPS (GPU and KOKKOS packages) and HOOMD-blue built for this GPU and checked against the same initial state.
